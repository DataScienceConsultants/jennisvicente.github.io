How I Work

I do not believe in dashboards that look impressive but change nothing.

The purpose of data is not to create more reporting. It is to help people notice what matters, understand why it matters, and decide what to do next.

My approach starts with the business question, the people using the solution, and the decision the work needs to support. The technology comes after that.

That same principle guides my work in lead generation, websites, operations, and business planning: clarify the goal, remove unnecessary friction, and build something people can realistically use.
