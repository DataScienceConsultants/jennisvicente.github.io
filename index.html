About Jennis

I didn’t write this from theory. I wrote it after living, losing, and rebuilding.

I lived through the emotional and financial realities many women are never fully prepared for. I learned what happens when love, money, paperwork, credit, expectations, and sacrifice all collide — and what it takes to rebuild with clarity.

After rebuilding my life alongside my husband and stepping away from a high-paying technical career, I chose a life closer to family in Puerto Rico — one built with more intention, honesty, peace, and clarity.

Now I write and speak for women who are ready to ask better questions before, during, and after marriage — not from fear, but from wisdom.
